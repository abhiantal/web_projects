1. How to Delete Your Account

NextGen provides two straightforward mechanisms to completely delete your Campus Circle account and all associated academic records:

Method A • In App

Inside the Portal / App

If you have active access to Campus Circle:

  1. Open Campus Circle
  2. Click on your Student Profile
  3. Select Security & Data
  4. Scroll down and click Delete Account & Purge Records
  5. Enter your confirmation password and confirm

✓ Account credentials and submissions scheduled for deletion.

Method B • Web Desk

Without Access

If you have lost your credentials or graduated:

Use the secure web submission form below. Our automated identity verification team will process your request and permanently scrub your records from cloud clusters.

Jump to Web Form →

2. Online Deletion Request Form

Enter the registered email associated with your Campus Circle account to request permanent deletion.

3. Data Types Permanently Erased

When an account deletion request is finalized, the following records are permanently erased from our cloud environments:

Category Records Deleted Status
Student Credentials & Profile Student ID, university roll number, email address, password hashes, and profile bio. Purged completely
Assignments & Lab Work Submitted assignment code, practice solutions, lab notes, and personal grades. Purged completely
Project Hub & Capstone Draft capstone proposals, team affiliations, and individual documentation files. Purged completely
Peer Collaboration & Messages Direct campus peer messages, team channel logs, and forum question replies. Purged completely
Storage Files & PDFs All uploaded documents, assignment attachments, and research files in cloud storage. Purged completely

4. Deletion Timeline

  • Profile Deactivation: Your account is disabled across all campus hubs immediately.
  • Attachment Scrub: Uploaded assignment files and research papers are purged within 7 days.
  • Database Cascade: All relational database foreign keys and academic records are scrubbed within 30 days.

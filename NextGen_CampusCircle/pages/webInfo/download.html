How to Install on Android

Android safeguards your device by asking for permission before opening downloaded APK files. Follow these simple steps:

1

Download APK

Tap the Download APK button above. If your browser displays a notice saying "File might be harmful", tap "Download anyway" (standard Android prompt for direct APK releases).

2

Open Package

Once the download completes, tap the download notification banner at the top of your screen, or open your phone's Downloads folder and tap campuscircle-v1.0.0.apk.

3

Allow Unknown Apps

If prompted with "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps", simply tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source".

4

Install & Launch

Tap the back button, then tap Install. In a few seconds, installation will finish. Tap Open to start exploring your university academic hub!

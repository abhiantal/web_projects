✔ profiles: Role-based user authentication
✔ subjects: Dynamic curriculum mapping
✔ exams: Timetables & seating records
✔ assignments: Problem statements & rubrics
✔ submissions: Encrypted student uploads
✔ pyqs: Verified question paper vault
✔ practice_questions: Interactive question bank
✔ projects: Showcase & upvote counter

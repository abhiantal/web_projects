Academic Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026 • Campus Circle by NextGen

1. Scope & Academic Commitment

Campus Circle ("we", "our", or "the Platform"), operated under NextGen Technologies, is committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of university students, professors, and academic contributors.

2. Data We Collect

We strictly collect only the information required to facilitate academic collaboration and resource discovery:

  • Authentication Data: University email and user role (Student, Faculty, Admin).
  • Academic Profile: Enrolled degree, branch, semester, and optional bio.
  • Deliverables: Submitted assignment files, GitHub repository URLs, and student project showcases.

3. Database Security & Row Level Security (RLS)

All student submissions, grades, and bookmarks are backed by encrypted cloud PostgreSQL with strict Row Level Security (RLS). Students can only view their own submissions, and faculty members can only evaluate coursework for their enrolled courses. We never sell, monetize, or track your academic data across third-party advertisers.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For questions regarding data privacy or to request account deletion, reach out through the verified campus support desk or contact NextGen Technologies privacy team.

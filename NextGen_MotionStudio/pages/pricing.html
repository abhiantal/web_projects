Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MotionStudio licensing and hardware rendering.

Do my video files get uploaded to the cloud?

No. Unlike Canva, Descript, or Runway, MotionStudio runs 100% inside your browser using client-side WebCodecs and WebGPU. Your media never leaves your computer.

Can I monetize videos rendered in MotionStudio?

Yes! All videos rendered on Pro Studio, Lifetime Pass, and Team plans include full unencumbered commercial ownership for YouTube monetization, client ads, and App Store promo reels.

What browsers support 4K 60FPS WebCodecs export?

Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera, and any Chromium-based browser supporting the W3C WebCodecs VideoEncoder specification with hardware acceleration enabled.

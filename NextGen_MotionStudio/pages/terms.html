ARTICLE 2

2. Zero-Cloud Privacy Guarantee

MotionStudio is architected with a strict privacy-first principle. Unlike cloud editing suites, your raw screen recordings, audio files, photos, and project assets remain stored exclusively in your browser's local memory (IndexedDB/WebCodecs).

No telemetry tracks your timeline contents. No video data is transmitted to external telemetry servers. You can run MotionStudio completely offline.
